An open itinerary for Nanyu
2018-03-12 · Fictional example

Only the fixed plans belong on this page. We can decide the rest as we go.

Morning
Have breakfast before leaving. Follow the road from the lodging to the old pier and stop at any shop that catches our attention.

Afternoon
Bring fruit back from the market. If it rains, sort photographs indoors. If it clears, go to the shore.

Before leaving
Choose one photograph of each travel companion and add the date. Keep the waiting and shared meals as well as the scenery.

Crossed out
Visiting three places in a single day. We decided to leave the second afternoon open.
