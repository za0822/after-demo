The Sunday soup that finally worked
2026-02-15 · Fictional example

What was left in the fridge
Potatoes, a carrot, half an onion and a few mushrooms. I used what was already there instead of shopping for a perfect list.

What worked this time
Turn the heat down and give the onion more time. Taste after the potatoes soften; do not add all the salt at the start.

At the table
I saved one bowl in the fridge for tomorrow’s lunch. Rain began outside just as I finished washing the pot.

For next time
Cut the pieces to a more even size and find a larger bowl.
