Notes from a gallery visit with Xu An
2023-11-23 · Fictional example

At the entrance
We stopped at the same painting but liked different parts. Xu An noticed the color at the edge; I first noticed the empty space in the middle.

The second room
We sat on a bench for a long time without reading the label. Each of us wrote one sentence, then we exchanged notebooks.

A question on the way out
If you could remember only one work, which would it be? We left the answer unwritten and agreed to ask again in a week.

Beside the ticket
Bring a smaller notebook next time. This one was a little heavy to carry.
