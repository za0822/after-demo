A reading list shared with Lin Xia
2019-01-20 · Fictional example

This is not a reading assignment, just a few ways of reading that came up when we met.

Lin Xia’s suggestion
Look at the contents first and begin with the chapter that interests you. It is fine to return a book unfinished. Tell me where you stopped next time.

My suggestion
Take a slim book while waiting for the bus. Copy favorite lines into your own notebook and leave the corners of borrowed pages unfolded.

Next exchange
An essay collection about ordinary observations, a wordless picture book, and one short piece chosen by each of us.

Note at the back
If a page reminds you of something, I would rather hear that story than how much you finished.
