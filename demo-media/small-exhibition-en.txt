A small exhibition draft
2019-06-02 · Fictional example

Working title: keeping an ordinary day.

What to show
One desk, three everyday records and an unsent letter. Visitors can read in their own order.

Space
Leave a pause at the entrance. Put photographs near eye level and words within reach. Sound plays only when someone chooses it.

To finish
Choose the photographs, check type sizes and walk through the whole sequence. Leave a blank card for the memory each visitor takes away.
