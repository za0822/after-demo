A picnic list for two
2024-06-08 · Fictional example

Lin Xia will bring
Cut fruit, two cups, and a cloth to sit on.

I will bring
Sandwiches, hot water, and a small bag to take our rubbish home.

We agreed
Find a place in the shade and do not chase the right time for photographs. Afterwards we can walk or do nothing at all.

Added after returning
The thing we forgot was a packet of tissues. We bought one at a nearby shop and sat for another ten minutes. Put it first on the list next time.
