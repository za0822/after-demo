Notes from my first design class
2016-06-10 · Fictional example

Assignment: express an everyday gesture on a single sheet of paper.

Observation
Before lifting a cup, people often move something aside. Empty space is part of the gesture.

Three attempts
1. Draw only the path of the hand.
2. Use folds to mark pauses.
3. Leave the shape of the cup blank.

Next time
Remove one element and look again. Keep the unsuccessful drafts: they explain the final choice.
