Three corners in Dukou
2017-05-27 · Fictional example

Morning | The bench by the ferry
A patch of wood had faded almost white in the sun. I put my bag beside me and realized there was no reason to hurry.

Lunch | The shop at the corner
We ordered hot soup. The owner saved us the window seats. I tucked the receipt into my notebook and drew the route back on its reverse.

Evening | Steps by the water
When our legs grew tired, we sat down. We did not visit every mark on the map, but I remember these three corners.

For next time
A reliable pen, a lighter bag, and a postcard to send home.
